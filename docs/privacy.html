1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Caleb Pierce, doing business as cfwebdev ("cfwebdev", "I", "me") handles information in connection with the website at cfwebdev.net and the website design and development services offered through it. cfwebdev is a sole proprietorship based in Orlando, Florida, United States.

2. What the website collects: nothing

This is a static website. Browsing it does not create an account, a profile, or a record held by cfwebdev. Specifically, this site:

  • Sets no cookies of any kind, including no analytics, advertising, or preference cookies. You will never see a cookie banner here, because there is nothing to consent to.
  • Runs no analytics or measurement service. There is no Google Analytics, no pixel, no beacon, and no visitor counter.
  • Loads no third-party scripts, fonts, embeds, or trackers. Every asset comes from cfwebdev.net itself, so your visit is not shared with an outside company as a side effect of loading the page.
  • Contains no contact form and no file upload. There is no field on this website that submits your data anywhere.
  • Does not use local storage or session storage to profile you.

The small amount of JavaScript on this site handles the mobile menu and the copyright year. It sends nothing anywhere.

Demonstration pages

The portfolio concept pages, such as the Cypressline landscaping concept, are fictional design demonstrations, not real businesses. Any form shown on a concept page is deliberately non-functional: it does not submit, transmit, or store what you type. It clears locally and tells you nothing was sent. Please do not enter real personal information into a demonstration form on any site, including this one.

3. What I collect when you contact me

The only way this website starts a conversation is by you choosing to email or call. The contact buttons open your own email application with a prefilled message; nothing is transmitted until you press send.

When you contact me, I receive and retain what you chose to send, which typically includes your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, your business or website details, and anything else you write.

Why I hold it (lawful basis): to respond to your enquiry and to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and where relevant to perform that contract. In GDPR terms this is Article 6(1)(b), with legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) covering ordinary business correspondence and record keeping.

I do not use your contact details for marketing lists, newsletters, or automated outreach, and I do not add you to any mailing system.

4. Client project data

If we work together, you may give me access to material needed to do the job, such as website content, images, brand assets, copy, and in some cases credentials to a hosting account, domain registrar, or content management system.

  • I use that access only to perform the agreed work.
  • Credentials are used for the engagement and are not shared with anyone else. You should change or revoke shared credentials after the project ends, and I will tell you when access is no longer needed.
  • Your domains, hosting, and third-party accounts remain yours and under your control throughout.
  • If your website handles data about your own customers, you remain the controller of that data. I act on your instructions in relation to it.

5. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through Cloudflare, Inc., which provides HTTPS, caching, and security protection. Like any web host, these providers automatically process technical request data such as IP address, browser user agent, requested URL, and timestamp, for the purpose of serving the page, preventing abuse, and maintaining security.

This is standard infrastructure logging performed by those providers under their own privacy policies. cfwebdev does not build an analytics profile from it and does not use it to identify or track individual visitors. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Email sent to cfwebdev.net addresses is handled by Namecheap Private Email, which stores and delivers that mail under its own privacy policy.

6. Third parties and outbound links

I do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing. I share information only where it is necessary to do the work you asked for, or where the law requires it.

This site links to external destinations such as GitHub, Steam, and client websites. Once you follow an outbound link you are on someone else's website, governed by their privacy policy, not this one.

Artificial intelligence tools

I use AI-assisted tooling as part of my development process, and I personally review every deliverable before it reaches you. I do not feed your credentials, private client data, or confidential business material into public AI services for training purposes. If a project requires a specific restriction on AI use, tell me and I will honour it in writing.

7. How long information is kept

Correspondence and project records are kept only as long as there is a reason to keep them: to deliver the work, to support it afterwards, to handle disputes, and to meet tax and accounting obligations. When a record no longer serves those purposes, it is deleted. You may ask me to delete your enquiry at any time, subject to any record I am legally required to keep.

8. Your rights

You may ask me to:

  • tell you what information about you I hold;
  • provide a copy of it;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete it, where I am not required to keep it;
  • restrict or object to a particular use.

Email caleb@cfwebdev.net and I will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, the right to request deletion, and the right to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. cfwebdev does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA and CPRA, and has not done so in the preceding twelve months. Because no sale or sharing occurs, no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism is required.

This website does not respond to Do Not Track signals, because it does not track you in the first place.

9. Children

These services are offered to businesses and adults. This website is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

10. Where information is handled

cfwebdev operates in the United States, and information you send is processed there. If you contact me from outside the United States, you understand your information will be handled in the United States, where data protection law may differ from your own.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date. Material changes to how information is handled will be reflected clearly rather than quietly. The date at the top always tells you which version you are reading.
