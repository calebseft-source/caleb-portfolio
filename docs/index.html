Before you book

Clear answers, before the work starts.

Does the $295 rescue cover any website problem?

No. It covers one existing page and three agreed front-end fixes. I review the page first. Backend failures, full redesigns, migrations, and major new features require a separate scope.

What do you need from me?

Usually your website URL, the action visitors should take, existing copy and brand assets, and site access after we agree on the scope. The project clock begins after the required material is ready.

Do you use templates?

I may use established frameworks, components, or platform features when they make the result faster, safer, and easier to maintain. The layout and implementation are tailored to the agreed goal, and I tell you what the site is built with.

Can you guarantee more leads or sales?

No honest web professional can guarantee sales from a page alone. I improve clarity, usability, responsiveness, and the path to contact. Results also depend on traffic, the offer, reputation, price, and follow-up.

How many revisions are included?

Both launch offers include one revision round. A revision adjusts the agreed direction; new pages, features, or a new direction are quoted separately.

Who owns the domain, hosting, and finished work?

Your domain, hosting, and third-party accounts remain in your control. After final payment, you receive the agreed project files and rights to custom deliverables. Third-party software, fonts, and assets keep their original licenses.

What happens after launch?

I correct defects in the agreed deliverables reported within 14 days of launch. New content, features, and ongoing maintenance can be scoped separately.
